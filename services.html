Services

Penn-Ohio Computer offers a range of services designed to support, improve, or simplify existing technology environments.

General services

Support for homes and small businesses, with an emphasis on reliability and clarity.

  • Network troubleshooting and performance optimization
  • Equipment reconfiguration and cleanup
  • New-owner consultation and system walkthroughs
  • Hardware removal and replacement
  • Basic computer support and diagnostics

Ubiquiti UniFi systems

Penn-Ohio Computer has extensive experience with Ubiquiti UniFi systems and commonly works with UniFi gateways, switches, and access points.

Support includes initial configuration, optimization, expansion, troubleshooting, and adapting existing UniFi installations to better fit current needs.
